Kinlivo Android 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-28 · 运营主体：Better Day USA Inc. · 应用包名：cloud.icook.kinlivo

Kinlivo 帮助成年照护者整理亲友的健康档案、保险、就诊、待办、福利和家庭照护资料。我们按你选择使用的功能处理资料。请仅录入你有权处理的本人或亲友资料，并在邀请家人和转介机构前确认授权。

收集的资料与用途

  • 账号与圈子：邮箱、称呼、账号 ID、圈子名称、成员与邀请记录，用于验证码登录、账号管理和家庭协作。Android 登录使用邮箱验证码；与 iOS 共用的账号如已关联 Apple 登录，后端也保存该账号关联。
  • 亲友及照护档案：你主动填写或上传的姓名、出生日期、性别、语言、地址、电话、保险识别号、医生与药房、用药、过敏、病史、法律文书、就诊、提醒、待办及备注。它们用于保存档案、整理就诊资料与圈内协作。
  • 福利资格资料：你选择填写的居住地区、邮编、家庭、身份、收入、资产和保险等资料，用于展示福利项目的初步匹配与材料缺口。你可以不填写某项，结果会标明信息不足。Kinlivo 不据此替政府做批准决定。
  • 照片、文件和录音：你选择的照片、相机返回的照片、PDF 和你主动录制的 WAV 音频，用于识别和归档；原件及识别结果保存到所属圈子。麦克风仅在你启动录音并授权后使用。系统选择器只向 App 提供你选中的文件；App 不要求读取整个相册、通讯录、短信或设备定位。
  • 问题和处理结果：你提交的问题、相关档案上下文、AI 识别或回答、用户确认和修正，用于完成你请求的整理和查询。
  • 反馈与举报：你主动提交的说明，以及你明确选择附带的摘录，用于开发者核查与处理问题。系统不会为了反馈自动附加整份健康档案、照片或录音；请勿在说明中提交无关的敏感资料。
  • 活动与运行信息：后端会记录账户、圈子、记录变更、处理状态、时间、请求运行信息和功能使用统计，用于审计、故障排查、安全防护和改善功能。网络服务商处理连接所必需的 IP 地址等网络信息。我们不在 Android App 中嵌入广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方广告分析 SDK。

Google Gemini 与 AI 处理

你使用 AI 拍照读件、语音整理或档案问答时，相关照片、PDF、录音、问题和需要的档案上下文会由 Kinlivo 后端发送给 Google Gemini API 生成识别、整理或回答结果。第一次使用前 App 会展示说明并征求你的明确同意。不同意时不要上传供 AI 处理的内容。

AI 可能遗漏、误读或生成不准确内容。请核对原件；涉及写入档案的识别建议由你确认。Kinlivo 是家庭记录工具，不是医疗器械，不作诊断、处方或治疗建议，也不能替代医疗专业人员。Google 的处理适用其 Gemini API 服务条款及 隐私政策；本政策不作零留存的承诺。

存储、访问与对外披露

  • 业务接口使用 HTTPS。业务记录保存在 Cloudflare Workers/D1，媒体保存在 Cloudflare R2。中国大陆的已授权 JPEG 下载可通过腾讯云区域图片服务分发；PDF 和 WAV 维持 Cloudflare 媒体路径。图片访问仍须通过登录及圈子成员校验，并采用短期签名链接。
  • 同一圈子的成员可查看和共同维护该圈子的亲友记录。请仅邀请可信成员。具备合法运营权限的人员可能为你请求的支持、安全和服务维护而访问必要资料。
  • Google Gemini 为所述 AI 功能处理相关内容。邮箱服务供应商处理登录验证码邮件所需的邮箱与邮件信息。
  • 你主动导出或分享 PDF、图片等资料时，由你选中的应用或接收者接收文件。你选择向机构转介时，按同意页面勾选的资料及相关授权进行；直接拨号或打开外部网站时，外部服务适用其自身政策。
  • 我们不出售个人资料、不将资料用于定向广告。依法必须披露或为保护用户和服务安全时，可能披露必要信息。

本机安全

Android 登录凭据由 Android Keystore 保护的加密存储保存；App 禁止系统自动备份，避免凭据随备份复制。导出文件可能临时保存在 App 缓存，并由你选择是否分享；接收者保存的副本不受 Kinlivo 控制。请保护设备锁屏及邮箱安全。传输加密不等于端到端加密，Kinlivo 服务会处理你提供的资料。

保留、账号注销与数据删除

你可在 App 的「我的」发起注销，也可通过数据删除页面联系支持。当前账号注销会撤销会话、移除设备登记、退出所有圈子并清除账号邮箱、称呼和 Apple 关联标识。仍有其他成员的圈子会移交给成员；共享档案不会因一个成员注销而一并删除。仅剩本人而无人访问的圈子及其档案、媒体并没有自动清除期限。

删除档案可能先将其标为已删除；现有后端允许被标记删除的亲友记录在 30 天内导出，这并不表示第 30 天自动物理清除。反馈与举报、审计、媒体、备份与已导出副本也不会仅因界面记录消失而自动全部清除。功能使用原始事件按约 180 天汇总为日统计。其余资料依所提供服务和你提出的删除请求保留；目前未对全部类别实现统一自动清除期。

若要删除账号关联资料、某位亲友的档案、原件或失去访问权限的圈子资料，请按照删除说明提出具体请求。我们会核验身份及你对亲友或共享资料的处理权限，并告知处理范围、共享成员影响、依法必须保留的资料及处理结果。不要在首次邮件中附上完整保险号、病历、录音或证件。

未成年人及联系

Kinlivo 面向成年照护者，不面向未成年人直接使用或注册。成年人仅应在有权处理的范围内记录亲友资料。

Kinlivo Android Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026 · Better Day USA Inc. · cloud.icook.kinlivo

Kinlivo helps adult caregivers organize family care records. Only provide information you are authorized to handle and confirm that you may invite relatives or share a record with an organization.

Information and purposes

  • Email, display name, account ID, care-circle membership and invitations support email-code sign-in, account management and collaboration. Accounts are shared with Kinlivo for iOS and may include an existing Apple sign-in association.
  • Records you enter can include names, birth dates, sex, language, address, phone number, insurance identifiers, clinicians, pharmacies, medicines, allergies, conditions, legal documents, visits, reminders, tasks and notes.
  • Optional benefits details can include location, ZIP code, household and immigration information, income, assets and insurance. They support preliminary eligibility matches and missing-information checks; Kinlivo does not decide eligibility for a government agency.
  • Photos you choose or capture, PDFs and voice recordings you initiate are uploaded to process and retain in your care circle. Microphone access is requested for recording. System pickers provide the files you select; the app does not request access to your whole photo library, contacts, SMS or device location.
  • Your questions, relevant record context, AI results and confirmed corrections support the features you request.
  • Feedback and reports include the explanation you submit and excerpts you explicitly choose to attach, for developer review and handling. A report does not automatically attach whole health records, images or audio. Avoid unrelated sensitive details.
  • The backend records account and circle activity, record changes, processing status, timestamps, operational request information and feature-use metrics for auditing, troubleshooting, security and improving functionality. Network providers process connection data such as IP addresses. The Android app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party advertising analytics SDK.

Google Gemini processing

When you request AI photo/document reading, voice filing or record questions, the Kinlivo backend sends relevant images, PDFs, audio, questions and record context to Google Gemini API to produce recognition, filing or answer results. The app presents a disclosure and asks for explicit permission before first use. Do not upload content for AI processing if you do not agree.

Check AI results against the originals and confirm proposed record changes. Kinlivo is a family record tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment or replace a qualified healthcare professional. Google's handling is governed by its Gemini API terms and Privacy Policy. We do not promise zero retention.

Storage and disclosures

Our API uses HTTPS. Records use Cloudflare Workers/D1 and media uses Cloudflare R2. Authorized JPEG delivery to mainland China may use Tencent Cloud's regional image service with membership checks and short-lived signed URLs; PDFs and WAV files use Cloudflare. Members of a care circle can view and maintain its records. Authorized operations personnel may access necessary information for requested support, security and maintenance. Google processes content for the AI features above, and an email provider handles sign-in messages. Files you actively export or share go to your selected application or recipient. Referrals use the fields selected in the consent flow. External websites and phone services have their own policies. We do not sell information or use it for targeted advertising. Necessary disclosures may also occur as required by law or to protect users and the service.

Device protection

Android sign-in credentials are stored with encryption protected by Android Keystore. System app backup is disabled. Export files may remain temporarily in the app cache, and recipients may retain shared copies outside our control. Protect your device and email account. HTTPS is transport encryption, not end-to-end encryption; our service processes the information you submit.

Retention and deletion

Request account deletion in Me or through our data deletion page. The current account-deletion flow revokes sessions, removes registered devices and circle memberships, and clears email, display name and Apple identifiers. Circles with remaining members are handed over; their shared records are not erased when one member leaves. Circles with no remaining members, their records and media currently have no automatic purge deadline.

Deleting a relative's record can first mark it deleted; the backend permits exports for 30 days after that mark, which does not mean an automatic physical purge on day 30. Feedback and reports, audit records, original media, backups and exported copies are not all removed by a record disappearing from the interface. Raw feature events are rolled up into daily metrics after approximately 180 days. No universal automatic purge period has been implemented for every data category.

To request removal of associated information, a relative's record, original files or an inaccessible circle's data, follow our deletion instructions. We verify identity and authority over relative/shared records, then explain the scope, effects on remaining members, any legally required retention and the outcome. Do not attach full insurance identifiers, medical files, recordings or ID documents to an initial email.

Audience and contact

The app is for adult caregivers, not direct use or registration by minors. Better Day USA Inc.: info@mail.icook.cloud. Material policy changes will be reflected in the date and appropriate in-app notices.
