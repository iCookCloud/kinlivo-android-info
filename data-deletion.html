Kinlivo 账号与数据删除

Better Day USA Inc. · cloud.icook.kinlivo · 更新：2026-10-28

你无需重新安装 Kinlivo，也可在本页发起账号及关联资料删除请求。

发送 Kinlivo 账号及数据删除请求 · 仅删除指定资料并保留账号

如何提出请求

  1. 请从注册邮箱发信到 info@mail.icook.cloud；无法使用注册邮箱时，请说明情况，我们会提供替代身份核验步骤。
  2. 说明要「注销账号并删除关联资料」，或「保留账号、删除指定资料」，并提供圈子名称、亲友称呼以及资料范围即可。
  3. 请勿首次附上完整病历、保险号、录音或证件。支持人员会在核验身份及处理权限后，说明处理范围、对共享圈子成员的影响、依法必须保留的资料、预计处理时间和结果。

App 内入口和当前行为

「我的 → 注销账号」会要求确认。成功后撤销会话、移除推送设备与圈子成员关系，并清除账号的邮箱、称呼和 Apple 关联。仍有成员的圈子会移交；共享健康档案及原件不会随单个账号注销自动删除。仅剩自己的圈子、其档案和原件目前也不会因注销自动物理清除。

若你希望一并删除档案、照片、PDF、WAV、识别结果、反馈与举报或其他关联资料，请使用上方邮件入口明确提出。这也适用于已经注销、失去圈子访问权限或卸载 App 的用户。共享资料需要核实你代表亲友处理资料的权限并考虑其他成员的合法权利；不能承诺撤回别人已经导出或另行保存的副本。

保留说明

当前后台对亲友记录使用软删除，删除后 30 天内可导出；此窗口不是第 30 天自动物理删除的承诺。原始使用事件约 180 天后汇总。对于审计、原始媒体、备份和无人可访问的圈子，目前没有覆盖全部类别的自动清除期。具体删除请求的范围和完成情况由支持核验后回复；依法必须保留的类别、理由和期间会单独说明。

Kinlivo Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion here without reinstalling the app. Contact Better Day USA Inc. at info@mail.icook.cloud. You may request account and associated data deletion, or deletion of specified information while keeping the account.

  1. Email from your registered address. If that is unavailable, explain this so support can arrange an alternative identity check.
  2. State the type of request and identify the care circle, relative and information using ordinary names. Do not initially attach medical records, full insurance identifiers, audio or identity documents.
  3. After verifying identity and your authority over the information, support will explain the scope, effects on shared-circle members, any legally required retention, expected handling time and the outcome.

Current in-app behavior

Me → Delete account asks for confirmation, revokes sessions, removes registered devices and memberships, and clears email, display name and Apple identifiers. Circles with remaining members are handed over; shared health records and original files remain. An inaccessible sole-member circle is also not automatically physically erased. To include records, photos, PDFs, WAV files, AI results, feedback and reports or other associated information, use the email path above, including after account deletion, app removal or loss of circle access.

Retention and limitations

Deleted relative records can remain exportable for 30 days; that is not an automatic physical-purge deadline. Raw usage events are rolled up after approximately 180 days. There is no universal automatic purge period for audit records, original media, backups and inaccessible circles. Support confirms the scope and completion of a specific deletion request and explains any legally required retention. Shared records require authority checks and consideration of other members' rights. Copies already exported by recipients cannot be automatically recalled. See our Privacy Policy.
